Velvet Horizon Cover Studio — turn your own photos into a GTA 6-style cover: an 8-panel comic grid with a pop-out hero, a huge striped chrome gradient title with a neon script subtitle, a role label on every panel, a 5-star press-quote top strip, a tagline banner, a gold starburst seal, and a COMING SOON credit strip with barcode. Upload your own photos (or use the 8 bundled photographic demo portraits), drag to pan and zoom each face into place, switch between 3 layouts and 6 sunset colour grades, edit every word, and export a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. Fully offline — no photo ever leaves your device. No Rockstar Games logos, artwork or fonts are used anywhere.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 (or set `PORT`).

## How it works

1. The demo cover loads first: 8 golden-hour portraits as THE DRIVER, THE BOSS (hero), THE MECHANIC, THE CAPTAIN, THE RIDER, THE BRAINS, THE GETAWAY and THE LOOKOUT.
2. Upload your own photos — they replace the demo cast panel by panel, processed in your browser only.
3. Click a panel to select it, drag the photo to pan, use the sliders to set face position and zoom. Click any role tag to rename it.
4. Pick a layout (Hero center / Hero left / Hero top), a grade (Sunset, Vice, Neon, Golden Hour, Noir, Palm), and edit the title, subtitle, tagline, quote, publication and seal.
5. Choose an export size (16:9, 1:1, 4:5) and hit Export PNG. Portrait exports reflow the grid; the title, strips, seal and barcode are redrawn on canvas at full resolution.
